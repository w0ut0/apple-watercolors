# Data & Image License

## Images (`data/images/`)

The paintings are from the **USDA Pomological Watercolor Collection**. USDA
staff artists made them, mostly between 1886 and 1942. As works of the U.S.
federal government (17 U.S.C. §105), they are in the **public domain** in the
United States. Wikimedia Commons, where these files were downloaded from, also
marks them public domain.

You don't have to credit the source, but the National Agricultural Library asks
for this credit:

> U.S. Department of Agriculture Pomological Watercolor Collection. Rare and
> Special Collections, National Agricultural Library, Beltsville, MD 20705.

## Metadata (`data/paintings.jsonl`, `data/cultivars.json`)

Most metadata (cultivar, scientific name, origin, artist, date) comes from the
USDA catalogue records and is public domain or factual. Some descriptive text
comes from the Wikimedia Commons file pages. Text written by Commons editors is
available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Each record's `commons_url` points to its source page.
